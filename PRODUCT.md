# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Jack (agency owner) and his team of 2–8 people. Used daily by the team to manage leads, demos, and pipeline; Jack uses it to oversee operations and track performance. Internal tool — no public users, no onboarding friction needed. Users are agency professionals who expect their tools to match the quality of their own client work.

## Product Purpose

Kracked Sales is an internal sales command center for an email design agency. It surfaces live data from GoHighLevel (CRM/pipeline) and ClickUp (demo tasks) in a single interface — pipeline health, demo fulfillment tracking, KPI dashboards, inbox management, follow-up queues, and analytics. Success means: at a glance, the team knows exactly where every deal and demo stands, what needs attention, and what's on fire.

## Brand Personality

Sharp, precise, premium. Confident without being loud. The tool reflects the standard of work the agency sells — it should feel like something you built for yourself, not something you subscribed to.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS — purple gradients, hero metric cards, identical card grids, startup template energy
- Enterprise CRM — Salesforce/HubSpot density, grey corporate UI, built for procurement teams not humans
- Consumer mobile — large rounded corners, pastel colours, app-store-style friendliness
- Dark mode gamer — neon on black, glow effects, crypto/trading terminal aesthetics

## Design Principles

1. **Earn every pixel.** If an element isn't carrying information or guiding action, it shouldn't be there. Decoration is debt.
2. **Data is the UI.** The interface exists to surface the data clearly. Treat density as a feature, not a problem — but only for data that earns it.
3. **Confidence through consistency.** Every interaction, every spacing step, every label follows the same rules. Inconsistency signals fragility.
4. **Hierarchy by function, not decoration.** Visual weight maps to decision weight. The most important thing on any screen should be unmistakably the most important thing.
5. **Built for people who know what they're doing.** No hand-holding, no tutorials, no empty-state comfort text. Users are competent — the UI should assume that.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA minimum. Internal tool with known users — no specialized needs flagged. Standard keyboard navigation and sufficient color contrast apply.
